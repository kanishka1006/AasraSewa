# 🚑 AasraSewa - Project Workflow & Architecture

## 1. System Architecture
The system utilizes a modern **Micro-Frontend Architecture** with a centralized Backend "Brain".

*   **🖥️ Admin Control Center (Frontend):**
    *   **Tech:** Next.js, Tailwind CSS
    *   **Role:** The Command Headquarters. Used by city officials to monitor live situations, manage resources, and simulate tactical scenarios (like crowd control).
*   **🧠 Central Intelligence (Backend):**
    *   **Tech:** NestJS, Google Gemini AI
    *   **Role:** The "Brain". Handles data processing, stores alerts in memory (Cache), coordinates between portals, and interfaces with Google Gemini for tactical AI analysis.
*   **🚁 Field Rescue Unit (Rescue Portal):**
    *   **Tech:** Next.js, Framer Motion, Leaflet Maps
    *   **Role:** Mobile interface for First Responders (Ambulance/Police). Receiving real-time dispatches, AI strategy, and live feed data.

## 2. Operational Workflow (The "Stampede" Scenario)
This is the life-cycle of a single rescue mission within AasraSewa:

### **Phase 1: Detection & Simulation (Admin Side)**
1.  **Incident Reporting:** Admin selects **"New Mission"** > **"Stampede / Crowd Control"**.
2.  **Visual Simulation:** Admin clicks specific points on the map to generate a **Crowd Heatmap**, simulating high-density areas.
3.  **Context Gathering:** The system automatically fetches:
    *   📍 **Reverse Geocoding:** Converts coordinates to a readable address (Nominatim API).
    *   🌤️ **Live Weather:** Fetches Wind Speed & Temp (Open-Meteo API).

### **Phase 2: AI Strategic Analysis (The "Brain")**
4.  **AI Request:** Backend sends the *Incident Type*, *Location*, *Details*, and *Weather Data* to **Google Gemini 2.0 Flash**.
5.  **Tactical Generation:** Gemini generates a **4-Point Tactical Response Plan**, prioritizing safety based on dynamic factors (e.g., "Use upwind entry due to high wind").

### **Phase 3: Dispatch & Alert (Communication)**
6.  **Dispatch:** Admin reviews the AI Plan and clicks **"Dispatch Crowd Control"**.
7.  **Data Transmission:** The Backend creates an Alert record containing the Mission Details + Heatmap Data + AI Strategy and broadcasts it.

### **Phase 4: Field Response (Rescue Portal)**
8.  **Alert Reception:** The Rescue Portal (polling the backend) flashes a **"Incoming Dispatch"** alarm.
9.  **Situational Awareness:** The Rescue Team opens the mission and sees:
    *   🗺️ **Satellite Map:** Shows the crowd heatmap overlay.
    *   🚁 **Drone Uplink:** A simulated live video feed with "Computer Vision" tracking people and panic levels.
    *   🤖 **AI Strategy:** The text plan generated by Gemini.
10. **Audio Briefing:** The Team Lead typically clicks **"Play Briefing"** (Text-to-Speech) to hear the plan while en route.
11. **Deployment:** Team clicks **"Confirm Deployment"**. The alert is removed from the system, and the mission is marked active.

## 3. Key Technological Features
*   **Generative AI Integration:** Uses Google Gemini for creating bespoke rescue strategies, not just generic templates.
*   **Simulation Engine:** Visualizes invisible data (crowd density) for better decision-making.
*   **Real-time External Data:** Integrates Live Weather APIs to make AI smarter.
*   **Accessibility:** Text-to-Speech integration for hands-free operation in high-stress environments.
